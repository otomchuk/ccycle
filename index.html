Everything you need to know about your cycle

🌑
Menstrual

Day 1 of your cycle. Estrogen and progesterone drop — a natural invitation to slow down and restore.

  • Rest and warmth are restorative
  • Gentle yoga and slow walks
  • Iron-rich foods support you
🌒
Follicular

Estrogen rises and energy builds. Your most creative, optimistic phase — ideal for new beginnings.

  • Start new projects and habits
  • Increase workout intensity
  • Social plans feel effortless
🌕
Ovulatory

Peak estrogen, peak confidence. Your fertile window — when communication and charisma shine brightest.

  • Schedule important conversations
  • High-intensity workouts feel best
  • Anti-inflammatory foods support you
🌖
Luteal

Progesterone rises. A time for completing, organising, and — in the final days — gentle self-care through PMS.

  • Swap HIIT for pilates or walks
  • Magnesium eases symptoms
  • Protect your energy and sleep

Private, personal,
and beautifully simple

📅
Dedicated calendar

Creates a separate "My Cycle" calendar so your phases stay beautifully organised, away from work and personal events.

🔒
Completely private

No servers. No accounts. No tracking. Your data lives only on your device and in your own Google Calendar.

🌍
English & Ukrainian

Full support for both languages — UI, event titles, and all phase descriptions. Switch at any time.

📖
Rich event descriptions

Each event includes what's happening hormonally, how you may feel, and practical tips for nutrition, exercise, and mood.

🎨
Colour-coded phases

Pink, coral, teal, and purple — each phase has its own distinct colour for instant visual recognition in your calendar.

🔄
Always up to date

Period started? Open the extension, update the date, and regenerate. Old events are replaced automatically.
